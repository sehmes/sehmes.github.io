My teaching focuses on economic inequality, labour markets, and unemployment from a comparative perspective, complemented by training in quantitative and cross-national research. I combine substantive questions of socio-economic stratification with rigorous methodological approaches to enable students to understand how inequalities are (re)produced in societies and how broader contexts shape these processes.

## Undergraduate Level (BA)

- Economic Inequality: Causes, Consequences and Policies (Goethe University Frankfurt)
- Introduction to Cross-national Social Research with Multilevel Modelling (Heidelberg University)
- Introduction to R for Social Science Data Analysis (Heidelberg University)
- Life Course and the Welfare State (Heidelberg University)
- Sociology of Labour Markets (Heidelberg University)
- Sociology of Unemployment (Heidelberg University)

## Graduate Level (MA)

- Empirical Research Training: International Comparative Social Research using Multilevel Analysis (with Bienstman) (Goethe University Frankfurt)
